A set of test images created from Sentinel-2 scenes using revised binary masks. The images have dimensions of 512 × 512 pixels and were generated from Sentinel-2 scenes covering the states of Minas Gerais and São Paulo, Brazil. They contain road samples and their corresponding binary masks for bands B2, B3, B4, B8, B11*, and B12*, with B11 and B12 resampled to a spatial resolution of 10 m, as well as derived indices computed from their combinations: NDVI, NDBI, NDWI, and the Road Index.

To access the complete dataset, click the link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/danielwander/s2-rd-lite
